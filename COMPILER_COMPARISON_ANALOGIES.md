# Traditional Compiler vs IPL: Analogy-Based Comparison

## TRAINING & LEARNING

### **Traditional Compiler**
```
Compiler = Driver driving a rental car
- Car is exactly as manufactured (no customization)
- Driver cannot modify the engine
- Every rental is identical
- No memory of previous trips
- No improvement over time
```

### **IPL**
```
Compiler = Driver driving a learned car
Training = Mechanic tunes the engine offline
.ipl_data/ = Garage stores tuned engine

- Mechanic learns from driving conditions
- Engine improves over time
- Car remembers what works
- Better performance with experience
- Customized for specific routes
```
